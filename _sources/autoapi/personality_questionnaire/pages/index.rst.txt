personality_questionnaire.pages
===============================

.. py:module:: personality_questionnaire.pages

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   The tab bodies of the data-collection application.

   Each module here builds elements and nothing else. Every decision these pages
   present -- whether a draft is ready, what remains unanswered, how a score is
   formatted -- is computed in :mod:`personality_questionnaire.core.state` or
   :mod:`personality_questionnaire.cli.render`, which are tested directly.

   That split is deliberate: these modules are excluded from coverage because their
   logic was extracted, not because it was hard to reach.
